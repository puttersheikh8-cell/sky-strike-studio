Privacy Policy.

Information about how this website handles data and how to contact us about privacy.

Last updated: October 18, 2026

1. Overview

Sky Strike Studio respects your privacy. This policy explains data practices for our informational website at skystrikestudio.site. Individual mobile games may have separate privacy policies reflecting the services and SDKs they use.

2. Information you provide

If you contact us by email, we receive the information you choose to share, such as your email address, message, and any attachments. We use it to respond to your inquiry and manage related correspondence.

3. Website hosting and technical data

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most hosting services, the provider may process technical information such as IP addresses and request logs for delivery, security, and reliability. See GitHub's privacy statement for its practices.

4. Cookies and analytics

This website does not intentionally set advertising cookies or run a dedicated analytics tracker. Third-party services, such as externally hosted fonts, may receive technical requests when you visit the site.

5. Sharing and retention

We do not sell information submitted through our contact email. We may share information with service providers where necessary to operate our email and website, or when required by law. Correspondence is kept as long as reasonably needed for the relevant purpose.

6. Your choices

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information you provided, subject to applicable law and legitimate retention needs.

7. Children

This website is intended to provide general studio information and is not designed to collect personal information from children.

8. Updates

We may revise this policy as our website and practices evolve. The latest version will appear on this page with an updated date.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, email contact@skystrikestudio.site.
